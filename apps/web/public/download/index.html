Download the app, open it, and allow the install. It takes about a minute. These apps are distributed by Loohar directly rather than through the Play Store.

Installing

  1. Tap Download above on the Android device itself.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will say it cannot install from this source.
  3. Tap Settings and turn on installs for the app you downloaded with, usually Chrome or Files. Then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open the app and sign in with your Loohar account.
The “unsafe” or “unknown source” warning is expected. Android shows it for every app installed outside the Play Store. It is not a sign that anything is wrong with the app. Only ever install a Loohar app downloaded from loohar.com.

Setting up the register

  1. Sign in as the owner or a manager the first time.
  2. The POS asks to register this device as a terminal. Give it a name such as “Front counter” and register it.
  3. Unlock the register with the cashier PIN, then open a shift with the opening cash.

Requirements

Android 7.0 or newer, and an internet connection. Roughly 5 MB per app. iPhone and iPad are not supported yet.
